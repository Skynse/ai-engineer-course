# Permissions and Ownership

**Video**: 1 min — Who can read, create, edit, and delete

---

Permissions control who can do what to your data. Without them, any visitor could create or delete any post.

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Anyone | Not logged in — can read public content |
| Authenticated | Logged in — can create posts and comments |
| Owner only | The author — can edit or delete their own content |

Appwrite enforces these at the collection and document level. Setting them correctly means your app is secure by default — not just hidden behind a frontend check.
